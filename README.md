# Analyzing Trust and Ethical Concerns of AI in Healthcare Systems

A comprehensive Capstone and Professional Practices research project investigating the multi-dimensional ethical, legal, and societal implications of deploying Artificial Intelligence (AI) within clinical and diagnostic environments. This study synthesizes public trust metrics with international professional engineering standards to propose safer, more accountable AI deployment models in medical fields.

## 🔬 Core Core Areas of Ethical Investigation

The research focuses on six key vulnerability domains in automated medical computing:
1. **The Black-Box Problem (Transparency):** Analyzing the critical lack of explainability in deep learning medical models making high-stakes diagnostic decisions.
2. **Diagnostic Risk & Accuracy Bounds:** Evaluating liability and error mitigation when automated systems fail or produce false positives/negatives in patient treatment tracks.
3. **Algorithmic Bias & Data Equity:** Investigating demographic underrepresentation and historical bias vectors within healthcare datasets (e.g., skewed clinical data models).
4. **Data Privacy & Telemetry Security:** Assessing security thresholds for medical imaging and patient files against global confidentiality standards.
5. **The Accountability Matrix:** Mapping legal and professional fault lines to determine ethical ownership of AI mistakes between software engineers, medical institutions, and attending clinicians.
6. **Automation Overdependence:** Quantifying the long-term cognitive risks of medical practitioners over-relying on algorithmic suggestions, leading to skill degradation.

---

## 🛠️ Theoretical Framework & Professional Standards

The core analytical architecture evaluates technological risk models against premier global professional governance frameworks:
- **Professional Codes of Conduct:** Cross-referenced system vulnerabilities against the **ACM Code of Ethics**, **IEEE Code of Ethics**, and **BCS (British Computer Society) Code of Conduct**.
- **Regulatory & Legal Matrix:** Analyzed international compliance benchmarks like **GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation)** and local statutory frameworks such as **PECA (Pakistan Electronic Crimes Act)**.

---

## 📊 Survey Methodology & Public Consensus

- **Empirical Instrument:** Deployed a structured, targeted multi-demographic survey evaluating user acceptance matrices for AI-driven disease diagnosis, surgical support, and autonomous prescriptions.
- **Key Discovery:** High willingness to adopt AI tools *only under strict human-in-the-loop (doctor supervision) architectures*, paired with extreme sensitivity toward data privacy and diagnostic algorithmic explainability.
